About Sidebar

The law is more than what fits in a textbook.

Sidebar began with a simple question: what does a legal career actually look like once you leave the classroom?

As a student with a growing passion for law, I wanted to hear directly from people who had already made the choices I am only beginning to consider — and to create a resource that other students could turn to as they navigate their own paths. Sidebar aims to give law students, aspiring lawyers, and anyone curious about the profession a clearer understanding of what truly goes into a career in law, through first-hand perspectives from the people who live it.

From the decisions that shape a career to the realities of day-to-day practice, these conversations look beyond titles and qualifications to explore what working in law actually means.

Sidebar is an attempt to document those conversations carefully and honestly — to capture the realities, uncertainties and experiences behind a profession that is too often reduced to a title.
